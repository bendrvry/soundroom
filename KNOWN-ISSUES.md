# Known issues in Soundroom 0.2.0

Things we know about. If you hit something not on this list, **⋯ › Send feedback**.

## Installing

- **It isn't signed by Apple yet**, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time (the install page has the steps).
- **Apple silicon only** (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or later.

## Playing

- **Bluetooth headphones add a delay** you can hear between pressing a key and the note. Use wired headphones or the built-in speakers.
- **The trackpad's Notes mode reaches an octave per slide.** At the 2 and 3 octave ranges you can press anywhere, but one slide stops following your finger past an octave.
- **The first moment after opening** can pause for a fraction of a second while the sand is laid out.

## Listen back

- **A take's sound can't be edited on the Sound rail yet.** Listen back plays each take in the sound it was played with; **Original sound** puts that sound on the selected track, where you can change it.

## The producer

- **The AI producer needs Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex** on your own subscription, connected from **Producer › Connect**. Soundroom works fully without it.

## Not yet

- No automatic updates: **New version** in the ⋯ menu opens the download page.
- No Windows, Intel Mac or iPad version.
