# Soundroom 0.2.0

The first version for friends.

Soundroom is a music studio for people who don't have one. Your laptop's letter keys are the instrument, every key is in the mood you pick so nothing clashes, and you shape the sound with sliders named by what you hear.

## In this version

- **Play, then press Enter** and what you just played becomes a loop, at the length you played it, with any bends and slider moves.
- **101 instruments and eight drum kits**, grouped into collections.
- **Shape the sound** with seven feel sliders, and Advanced for everything else: Hold, Wobble, Chop, Pump, Octave, Thin, Glide, and a Find field.
- **The trackpad** bends notes or plays them like a theremin that stays in key, with a range from a half-step to an octave.
- **Listen back** keeps everything you play, divided into takes that remember their instrument.
- **Record your voice** into a track, tuned to the song's key, with harmonies if you like.
- **Arrange** loops into sections and songs; export WAV, stems, MIDI, or straight into GarageBand.
- **An AI producer** if you use Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex: it can add a drum part that fits, shape a sound, and measure it. Everything it adds waits for you to keep it.

## Install

Download `Soundroom-0.2.0-arm64.dmg` below and follow the [install steps](https://github.com/bendrvry/soundroom#install). Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time.
