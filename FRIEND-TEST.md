# Trying Soundroom: a script for friends

Thanks for trying it. This takes about fifteen minutes. There are no wrong answers: if something is confusing, that's the app's fault, and it's exactly what Ben wants to hear about.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and wired headphones or the built-in speakers. Bluetooth headphones add a delay.

## 1. Install (3 minutes)

Follow the [install steps](README.md#install). Note anything that made you hesitate, especially the "Not Opened" box and Open Anyway.

## 2. Open it (1 minute)

Watch the opening, then press **Enter** (or click **Enter studio**).

## 3. Play (3 minutes)

Press letter keys. Every key is a note in the same mood, so nothing clashes. Try the top bar's mood (it says something like "Wistful in A") and pick another.

## 4. Keep something (3 minutes)

Play a short phrase you like, then press **Enter**. It becomes a loop that plays on its own. Then choose another track in the list and play something over it, and press Enter again.

## 5. Change the sound (3 minutes)

With a track selected, move the sliders on the right (Warmth, Haze, Grit…). Hold the **\\** key to hear it without your changes.

## 6. Take it away (2 minutes)

**Share ▾** exports a WAV of your song, or sends it to GarageBand.

## Then tell Ben

**⋯ › Send feedback** opens an email with the version filled in. Answer whichever of these you like:

1. Did anything during install make you want to give up?
2. What did you think Soundroom was, from the first screen?
3. Where did you get stuck, or have to guess?
4. Did you keep a loop? Did it come out the way you played it?
5. Would you open it again? What for?
